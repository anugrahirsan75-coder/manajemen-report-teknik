Option Explicit

' Lupa memasukkan ke aplikasi biasanya terjadi sesudah lembarnya selesai, jadi
' pengingatnya dipasang di dua titik akhir itu: saat dicetak dan saat ditutup.

Private Sub Workbook_SheetChange(ByVal Sh As Object, ByVal Target As Range)
    KirimSppbj.CatatBerubah Sh
    KirimSppbj.SebarkanMa Sh, Target
End Sub

Private Sub Workbook_BeforePrint(Cancel As Boolean)
    KirimSppbj.RapikanKakAktif
    KirimSppbj.IngatkanSebelumCetak
End Sub

Private Sub Workbook_BeforeClose(Cancel As Boolean)
    KirimSppbj.IngatkanSebelumTutup
End Sub
